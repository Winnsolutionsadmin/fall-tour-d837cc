What this is and is not. This is every Europe→US nonstop with SAVER/partner award space indexed by the award API in your window — the space bookable with transferable points. It is NOT a cash-flight schedule and it is NOT Delta dynamic pricing: Delta prices every cash seat in miles inside its own app (your 39k SMF find), and that surface is invisible to every tool here — for any Delta-metal flight below, also check the app for a dynamic price. Other caveats: seat count 0 means "not reported", not zero; taxes are shown in each program's own currency (a CAD figure burned this study once already); award space moves hourly, so treat any single row as "existed on 4 Aug", not a guarantee.
