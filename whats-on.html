THE ALIGNMENT: Ireland is where the event and the flight home agree. Two independent lanes converged on it. Derry Halloween (40th anniversary, Europe's largest, free) plus Púca at Samhain's literal birthplace are the strongest in-window events for these ages — and Dublin is an Aer Lingus hub, which makes it the cleanest points exit on the board: AerClub is a 1:1 Amex transfer partner and DUB→SFO economy was verified at 16,250 Avios with 6+ seats on both Nov 1 and Nov 2. Ryanair also sells Dubrovnik→Dublin on Oct 25 only — which is exactly the day you check out. The experience, the routing, and the award all point the same direction. The catch remains Derry lodging: a live census found one entire-home listing for five, and it was in Donegal.
Be honest about the window: it is a genuine dead zone for mega-events. Every UEFA competition is dark (Champions League matchday 3 is Oct 20–21, matchday 4 is Nov 3–4 — you fall exactly in the gap). Rugby's Nations Championship starts Nov 6. MotoGP's European finales moved to Nov 22 and Nov 29. F1 is in Mexico and Brazil. Web Summit is Nov 9–12, not Nov 2–5. Slush is Nov 18–19. Amsterdam Dance Event's 30th anniversary ran Oct 21–25 and is 18+. Nearly everything you would picture sits one to three weeks on either side of these dates. Three things carry the window: El Clásico, Ireland's Halloween, and Kraków on All Saints.
Where the flights point. The award research landed on Amsterdam as the best way home — KLM's AMS→SFO nonstop, 2 lie-flat + 3 economy for 195,000 points and ~$750 — with an all-cash British Airways backstop at $5,867 that is already verified bookable. But Amsterdam is a departure airport, not a destination: nothing above is happening there. The real question is which of these experiences you want, then whether its nearest airport has a decent exit. Barcelona, Paris, Dublin, London, Copenhagen and Kraków all have strong ones. Cappadocia, Petra and Cairo do not — they would need a positioning flight back into Europe.
Ibiza, answered honestly for the third time: no. Not a reduced programme — nothing. Every 2026 closing party fell in a ten-day window in early October: Amnesia Oct 1, Ushuaïa Oct 2, Hï Oct 3, Amnesia Glitterbox Oct 9. By Oct 25 the superclubs are shut for winter and much of the island's hospitality is closed. And separately from the season: Ibiza's clubs are 18+, so three of your five are barred from the only thing the island is famous for even in August. What remains is a pretty Mediterranean island with swimmable sea and a good old town — which will please everyone mildly and thrill nobody.
Two other honest negatives. There is no mega-tour in Europe that week — I checked hard. The Weeknd's stadium leg ended in September; what's left is nostalgia acts. And Amsterdam's marquee events bracket your window: ADE ran Oct 21–25, Museumnacht is Nov 7, the Light Festival opens Nov 26. Arriving Oct 26 means landing in the trough between all three.
