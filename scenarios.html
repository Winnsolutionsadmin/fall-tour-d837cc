What Halloween actually is, after checking. Dublin city has no Halloween parade — a 2024 AI-generated listing sent crowds to O'Connell Street for an event that never existed, and pages still repeat it. The Bram Stoker Festival is Oct 23–26, a literary festival, not Halloween. The real thing is Derry: Europe's largest Halloween festival, 40th anniversary, 100,000+ people, free carnival parade at 7pm on Oct 31 and fireworks over the Foyle at 8:15pm, plus an illuminated walled-city trail on Oct 30. Seventy miles from Dublin by coach. There is no late transport back on Oct 31 — if you watch the fireworks, you sleep in Derry, and those beds book months out.
Everything ends on the same flight. Aer Lingus EI61, Dublin → San Francisco nonstop with US preclearance, 16,250 Avios each plus $194.63 — verified bookable with six or more seats open on every date including Nov 1 and Nov 2. That is 81,250 of your 525,382 Amex points, and it is the only redemption on this trip that clearly beats paying cash. The rest of the pool stays banked; these are cheap economy fares and forcing points into them loses value.
