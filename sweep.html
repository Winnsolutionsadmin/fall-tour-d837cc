Zero · How this sweep actually works — read before trusting any bar

The speed question, answered honestly: nothing here is a live airline search. Every number is a query against a continuously-crawled award-availability database (seats.aero) that indexes 23 loyalty programs' award calendars. That is why 36×36×9 takes minutes — it is a database read, not 11,000 searches. The cost of that speed is staleness, and here is the actual distribution for the 2,171 outbound rows on this page: 19% observed within 24h · median age ~4 days · 5% older than 2 months. Per-door freshest observation is now printed on every card below — the St Lucia door is 83 days old and should be treated as dead until re-checked; Sint Maarten's 58.5k is 4.5 days old; the Canada doors are 6–28h old.

Also a correction to my own earlier wording: where I said Zurich/Munich were "re-verified live", the re-pull verified their presence in the current index — it did not query Aeroplan itself. The one true end-to-end confirmation in this study is that Jarred's own Aeroplan screenshot matched the Munich row to the dollar (40K + CA$258) — which validates the pipeline when rows are fresh, not every row.

What this instrument cannot see at all: cash fares · Delta app dynamic pricing (the 39k SMF find) · multi-passenger repricing on dynamic programs (Flying Blue) · BA/Virgin cash surcharges (rows show $0/low) · end-to-end bookability. The protocol that makes it safe anyway: nothing is transferred or booked until the program site's own cart shows the price at your passenger count — the cart is the live check. This page's job is to narrow 11,000 possibilities to the ~11 worth carting.
