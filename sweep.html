Why this pass found 11 doors where the first found 4: the first pass covered 18 gateways over 7 days, business-only, 5-seats-only, and skipped the Condor/JetBlue booking channel. This one covers double the gateways, adds Oct 25–26, all four cabins, every seat count, and prices the backhaul. Standing caveats: snapshot data (space moves hourly — cart before transferring), seat counts are airline-asserted (rows with unreported counts are excluded from 5-seat boards), JetBlue-booked rows cost 25% extra MR (1.25:1 transfer), and BA/Virgin ex-LONDON rows carry unshown cash surcharges (£300–720/pp) — they are tagged.
