02The two findings that matter more than any price

⚠️ The Dubrovnik 4-passenger capacity wall

Every non-Ryanair carrier out of DBV caps at 4 passengers. Tested explicitly: DBV→Zagreb prices at 1/2/3/4 pax ($128/$255/$382/$510) then returns zero itineraries at 5. Identical cliff on Frankfurt, Amsterdam, Paris, Madrid, Barcelona, Istanbul, Milan, Copenhagen, Athens, Munich, Zurich, Lisbon, Warsaw — and on the DBV→SFO through-ticket. It does not relax on Oct 27, Oct 28 or Nov 1.

Only Ryanair returns 5 seats: Vienna $295 · London STN $503 · London LGW $636 · Dublin $815 (all party-of-5).

Consequence: any legacy-carrier plan must be booked 4 + 1. A single award ticket (Aeroplan / Flying Blue) sidesteps this entirely — a second reason those win.

⚠️ November is NOT cheaper on cash

At true party-of-5 pricing, Nov 1–2 is a wash or worse: London identical both windows, Madrid identical, Amsterdam identical on all four dates, Barcelona identical. Paris is the lone exception — cheaper by $66. Extending a week buys ~$1,400–2,100 of extra lodging to save nothing.

November is justified only by award availability — never by cash fares.
