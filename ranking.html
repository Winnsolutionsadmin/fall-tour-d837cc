THE MADRID CORRIDOR (added 5 Aug — it tops both boards, and the positioning question is answered). Iberia ex-Madrid is the cheapest award metal across the Atlantic and its real-world fees are LOW (~$90/pp): MAD→LAX nonstop IB351 12:55→17:40 at 20,000/pp — 100,000 for all five (8 seats Wed Oct 28 · 5 seats Sun Nov 1 · 4 seats Oct 25), MAD→Boston/JFK/Chicago at 16,000/pp = 80,000 for five on 6+ dates, and the cheapest split on the board (MAD→IAD 2×59k + 3×19.5k = 176.5k). No business award space exists on the LAX nonstop — economy only. The business reality, checked both ways (5 Aug): the A330 does carry a 1-2-1 flat-bed cabin, but award J across ALL Madrid→US routes in the window totals 2 seats (IAD, Oct 30) + 1 seat (MCO, Nov 2) — nothing usable for any of your shapes — and cash business on IB351 prices at $12,500/pp. Madrid is the economy-value king and nothing else. If beds matter, they live in Frankfurt: 5 beds FRA→ATL 375k Aeroplan, or the splits — FRA→PHL 2+3 at 217.5k, FRA→JFK 2+3 on Singapore's A380 at 260.5k.

Getting there: NO Dubrovnik→Madrid nonstop operates in the window (verified live, 3 dates). Best connection: Croatia Airlines via Zagreb, 4h40 door-to-door, ~$1,072 for five on Mon Oct 26 or Thu Oct 29 (50-min ZAG connection — one ticket, protected). The assembled play: DBV→MAD Oct 26 ($1,072) → Madrid day → IB351→LAX Oct 28 (100,000 Avios + ~$450) → LAX→SMF hop (~$400/5). ≈ 100,000 points + $1,920 all-in — the fewest points of ANY family plan in this study, with a Madrid night thrown in. Transfer MR→Iberia 1:1 (or via BA, Avios move freely); book iberia.com; cart-check before transferring — Iberia off-peak space moves.
