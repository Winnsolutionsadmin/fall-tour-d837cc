⚠ AWARD SWEEP RETRACTED AGAIN — AND EVERY SURVIVING OPTION IS IN A PROGRAM YOU CANNOT REACH
The tool has a trap that invalidated the earlier tables. seats.aero's RemainingSeats field returns 0 to mean "the program did not report a count," not "no seats" — that state covers 18% of priced rows and it is volatile (the same Lufthansa flight read 6 seats at 07:50 and unreported at 09:10 with nothing sold in between). Two method corrections that affect how any of this is read:
· min_seats asserts against data seats.aero already holds — it does not force a refresh. A row can pass a 5-seat filter on two-day-old counts.
